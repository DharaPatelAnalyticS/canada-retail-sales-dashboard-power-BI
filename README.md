# Canada Retail Sales & Profitability Dashboard
![Canada Retail Sales Dashboard](./Screenshot%202026-09-19%20162952.png)
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail sales performance, profitability, customer activity, and order fulfillment across Canadian provinces.

## 📊 Key Business Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Sales:** $9M
* **Total Profit:** $3M
* **Total Orders:** 10K
* **Total Customers:** 1,987
* **Average Discount:** 6.07%
* **Average Achievement Rate:** 75.20%

## 🔍 Key Insights & Features

* **Salesperson Performance:** Analyzes sales distribution and performance among top salespeople, including Chloe Davis, Emma Wilson, and Mia Thompson.
* **Product Category Analysis:** Breaks down revenue across Electronics (24.97%), Furniture, Accessories, and Office Supplies.
* **Regional Analysis:** Provides interactive filtering by Canadian province, including Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Quebec, and Saskatchewan.
* **Order Fulfillment Analysis:** Tracks Completed, Pending, Cancelled, and Returned orders across major Canadian cities.
* **Product Profitability:** Identifies and ranks high-performing products, including Laptop Pro 14", Tablet 11", and Standing Desks.
* **Interactive Dashboard:** Uses Power BI visuals, filters, slicers, and KPI cards to support business-focused analysis and exploration.
